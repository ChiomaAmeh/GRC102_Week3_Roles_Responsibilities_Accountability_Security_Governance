# Consultant Justification
 
## Executive Summary
 
TechGlobal's current cybersecurity governance model is highly dependent on the IT Director and lacks formal governance structures. This creates accountability gaps, inconsistent decision-making, and limited visibility of cyber risk across executive leadership and the Board.

 
The proposed governance framework addresses these weaknesses by introducing Board oversight, executive accountability, formal governance committees, and enterprise-wide risk ownership.
 
---
 
## Why Board Oversight Is Required
Cybersecurity has evolved from a technical concern into a business risk. Material cyber incidents can affect company reputation, financial performance, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.
 
Board involvement ensures:
 
- Strategic oversight of cyber risk
- Approval of risk appetite
- Executive accountability
- Visibility into significant threats and incidents
 
---
 
## Why Executive Accountability Is Required
Executives are responsible for achieving organisational objectives while managing business risk.
 
The proposed structure ensures:
 
- Risk ownership is assigned appropriately
- Security decisions support business objectives
- Resources are allocated effectively
- Security performance is regularly monitored
 
---
 
## Why a Security Governance Committee Is Required 
The absence of a formal governance committee contributes to inconsistent security decisions across business units.
 
A Security Governance Committee provides:
 
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Formal decision-making
- Policy oversight
- Risk review and escalation
- Governance transparency
 
---
 
## Why Defined Roles and Responsibilities Are Required
Clearly documented responsibilities reduce confusion and improve accountability.
 
A structured responsibility framework ensures:
 
- Ownership of cyber risk
- Consistent governance practices
- Improved decision quality
- Reduced duplication of effort
 
---
 
## Why Formal Risk Escalation Is Required
Major cyber risks require timely visibility at both executive and Board levels.
 
A formal escalation process ensures:
 
- Rapid decision-making
- Appropriate management involvement
- Effective crisis management
- Consistent reporting procedures
 
---
 
## Conclusion
The proposed governance model transforms TechGlobal's informal IT-centric approach into a mature governance framework aligned with business objectives. The framework introduces accountability, transparency, risk ownership, and executive oversight necessary to support organisational growth and security resilience.
