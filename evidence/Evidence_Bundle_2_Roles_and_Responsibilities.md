# Evidence Bundle 2: Roles and Responsibilities
 
## Purpose
This evidence bundle establishes governance responsibilities, decision authority, accountability relationships, and conflict-resolution mechanisms for TechGlobal's security governance framework.
 
The bundle defines the responsibilities of key stakeholders involved in cybersecurity governance and ensures accountability is distributed across executive leadership, risk management, business functions, and technical operations.
