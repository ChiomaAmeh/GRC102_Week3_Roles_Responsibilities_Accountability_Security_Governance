# Security Governance Role Profiles
## Board of Directors
### Purpose
Provide strategic oversight for cybersecurity governance and risk.
 
### Responsibilities
- Approve cyber-risk appetite
- Review cyber-risk reporting
- Monitor governance effectiveness
- Hold executives accountable
 
### Decision Authority
Strategic and enterprise-level decisions.

---
 
## Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
### Purpose
Provide executive leadership and accountability.
 
### Responsibilities
- Sponsor cybersecurity governance
- Align security strategy with business objectives
- Allocate resources
- Review significant risks
 
### Decision Authority
Executive governance decisions

---
 
## Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
### Purpose
Lead cybersecurity governance and strategy.
 
### Responsibilities
- Develop security policies
- Report cyber risk
- Coordinate governance committees
- Oversee governance implementation
 
### Decision Authority
Security governance and policy management.

---
 
## Chief Risk Officer (CRO)
### Purpose 
Integrate cybersecurity into enterprise risk management.
 
### Responsibilities
- Monitor cyber risks
- Maintain risk register
- Support risk escalation
- Coordinate risk assessments
 
### Decision Authority
Risk management framework decisions.

---
 
## Legal Counsel
### Purpose
Manage legal and regulatory obligations.
 
### Responsibilities
- Provide legal guidance
- Review compliance requirements
- Support regulatory reporting
- Assess legal risks
 
### Decision Authority
Legal and compliance matters.

---
 
## Finance Director
### Purpose 
Manage financial oversight of cybersecurity investments.
 
### Responsibilities
- Review security budgets
- Approve funding requests
- Assess financial impacts of cyber risks
 
### Decision Authority
Financial approval authority.

---
 
## HR Director
### Purpose
Manage workforce-related security controls.
 
### Responsibilities
- Security awareness training
- Background screening
- Onboarding and offboarding
- Disciplinary procedures
 
### Decision Authority
Personnel-related matters.

---
 
## IT Director
### Purpose
Implement technical security controls.
 
### Responsibilities
- Manage infrastructure security
- Deploy security technologies
- Support incident response
- Implement approved controls
 
### Decision Authority
Technical implementation decisions.
