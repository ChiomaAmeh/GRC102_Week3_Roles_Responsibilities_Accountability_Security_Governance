# Sample Security Governance Committee Meeting Agenda

Meeting: Security Governance Committee

Frequency: Monthly

Duration: 90 Minutes

---
 
## Agenda
### 1. Opening Remarks
Review meeting objectives.

---
 
### 2. Review Previous Minutes
Confirm action items and previous decisions.

---
 
### 3. Enterprise Risk Dashboard
Review:
- Risk ratings
- Emerging threats
- Risk treatment progress
 
---
 
### 4. Security Incidents
Review:
- Recent incidents
- Root-cause analysis
- Corrective actions
 
---
 
### 5. Governance and Policy Review
Review:
- Security policies
- Standard updates
- Regulatory changes
 
---
 
### 6. Compliance Update 
Review:
- Audit findings
- Compliance programmes
- Remediation actions
 
---
 
### 7. Security Investments
Review:
- Budget requests
- Resource requirements
- Strategic initiatives
 
---
 
### 8. Escalations
Review risks requiring Executive Committee review.

---
 
### 9. Action Items
Assign responsibilities and timelines.

---
 
### 10. Close
