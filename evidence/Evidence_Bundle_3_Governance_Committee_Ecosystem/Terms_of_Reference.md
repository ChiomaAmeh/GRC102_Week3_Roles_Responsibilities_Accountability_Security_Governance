# Security Governance Committee Terms of Reference
## Committee Name
Security Governance Committee (SGC)
 
---

## Purpose
The Security Governance Committee provides enterprise-wide oversight of information security governance activities and supports risk-informed decision-making.

---

## Objectives
- Monitor cybersecurity risks.
- Review governance performance.
- Approve policy recommendations.
- Coordinate risk treatment activities.
- Escalate significant risks.
- Support compliance initiatives.
 
---
 
## Membership
### Chair
Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
 
### Members
- Chief Risk Officer
- Legal Counsel
- Chief Financial Officer
- Human Resources Director
- IT Director
- Business Unit Representatives
 
---
 
## Meeting Frequency
Monthly
Additional meetings may be called following major cyber incidents or regulatory developments.
 
---
 
## Decision-Making Authority
The committee may:
- Recommend policy approval.
- Review governance performance.
- Approve remediation plans.
- Escalate strategic risks.
 
---
 
## Reporting
The committee reports to the Executive Risk Committee and provides quarterly governance updates.

---
 
## Review Cycle
Terms of Reference shall be reviewed annually.
