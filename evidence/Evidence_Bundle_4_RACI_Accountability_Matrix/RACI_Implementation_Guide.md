# RACI Matrix Implementation Guide
## Purpose
The RACI Accountability Matrix is a governance tool designed to clarify ownership, decision authority, consultation requirements, and reporting responsibilities across security governance activities.
 
The matrix supports effective governance by ensuring that every activity has clearly assigned accountability.

---
 
# Implementation Guidance
## Step 1: Identify the Governance Activity
Before any governance activity begins, management should identify which governance process is being performed.
Examples include:
- Risk assessments
- Policy development
- Incident management
- Security awareness initiatives
- Regulatory compliance reviews
 
---
 
## Step 2: Confirm the Accountable Owner
Every activity must have one Accountable (A) individual.
The accountable owner:
- Approves outcomes.
- Makes final decisions.
- Owns performance results.
- Ensures objectives are achieved.
 
Accountability cannot be shared.
 
---
 
## Step 3: Assign Responsible Personnel
Responsible (R) individuals perform the operational work required to complete the activity.
Examples include:
- Conducting assessments
- Writing policies
- Implementing controls
- Managing remediation plans
 
---
 
## Step 4: Engage Consulted Stakeholders
Consulted stakeholders provide expert input before decisions are made.
Examples include: 
- Legal Counsel
- Finance Director
- CRO
- HR Director
 
Consultation improves decision quality and reduces governance risk.
 
---
 
## Step 5: Inform Relevant Stakeholders
Informed stakeholders receive updates after decisions have been made.

This ensures transparency across governance functions and maintains appropriate oversight.

---
 
# Governance Benefits
Implementation of the RACI model provides:
- Improved accountability
- Better governance transparency
- Reduced duplication of effort
- Faster decision-making
- Enhanced stakeholder engagement
- Stronger risk management
 
---
 
# Review Requirements
The RACI matrix should be reviewed:
- Annually
- Following organisational restructuring
- After significant cybersecurity incidents
- When governance responsibilities change
- As part of governance maturity assessments
 
---
 
# Success Factors
Successful implementation depends on:
1. Executive sponsorship.
2. Clear communication.
3. Governance training.
4. Regular review.
5. Consistent application.
 
---
 
# Conclusion

The RACI Accountability Matrix is a foundational governance tool that supports effective decision-making, accountability, and oversight across TechGlobal's security governance framework. When consistently applied, it strengthens governance maturity and helps ensure cybersecurity responsibilities remain aligned with organisational objectives.
