# Major Cyber-Risk Escalation Workflow
## Purpose
This workflow establishes the process for escalating cybersecurity risks and incidents through TechGlobal's governance structure.

---
 
## Escalation Workflow
```text
Cybersecurity Event Detected
                │
                ▼
Security Operations Team
                │
                ▼
Initial Risk Assessment
                │
                ▼
Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
                │
      ┌─────────┼─────────┐
      │         │         │
      ▼         ▼         ▼
Operational  Executive  Board-Level
Escalation   Escalation Escalation
      │         │         │
      ▼         ▼         ▼
IT Director    CEO    Board Risk and
                      Audit Committee
```
 
---
 
## Escalation Process
### Stage 1: Event Detection
Cybersecurity risks or incidents may be identified through:
- Security monitoring tools
- Threat intelligence feeds
- Vulnerability assessments
- Internal audits
- User reporting
- Third-party notifications
 
---
 
### Stage 2: Initial Assessment
The Security Operations Team evaluates:
- Severity
- Scope
- Systems affected
- Data affected
- Business impact
- Regulatory requirements
 
---
 
### Stage 3: CISO Review
The CISO determines:
- Risk classification
- Escalation level
- Required response actions
- Stakeholders requiring notification
 
---
 
### Stage 4: Management Escalation
Based on impact, the issue is escalated to:
- IT Director (Operational)
- CEO and Executive Leadership (Executive)
- Board Risk and Audit Committee (Material Risk)
 
---
 
### Stage 5: Resolution and Reporting
Management receives periodic updates until:
- Risk is mitigated
- Incident is resolved
- Root-cause analysis is completed
- Closure is approved
 
---
 
## Expected Outcomes
- Faster executive decision-making
- Improved accountability
- Better risk visibility
- Consistent incident reporting
- Stronger governance oversight
