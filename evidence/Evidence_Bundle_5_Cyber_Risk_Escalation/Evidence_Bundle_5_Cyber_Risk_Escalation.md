# Evidence Bundle 5: Cyber-Risk Escalation
## Purpose
This evidence bundle establishes a structured cyber-risk escalation framework for TechGlobal.

The framework ensures significant cybersecurity risks and incidents are identified, assessed, escalated, and communicated to the appropriate level of management based on business impact, financial exposure, regulatory implications, and reputational risk.

## Overview
Effective cyber-risk escalation ensures decision-makers receive timely information regarding significant risks and incidents.
 
The escalation model proposed for TechGlobal establishes clear reporting lines between Security Operations, Executive Management, and the Board Risk and Audit Committee to support informed decision-making and organizational resilience.
