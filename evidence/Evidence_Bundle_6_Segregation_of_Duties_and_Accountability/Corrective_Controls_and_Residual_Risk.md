# Corrective Controls and Residual Risk Analysis
## Purpose
This document identifies recommended corrective controls for the weaknesses identified within TechGlobal's governance model and evaluates the anticipated residual risk after implementation.

---
 
# Corrective Control Register
| Weakness ID | Recommended Corrective Control | Residual Risk |
|-------------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| W1 | Establish Security Governance Committee and Board oversight | Medium |
| W2 | Separate approval, implementation, and review responsibilities | Low |
| W3 | Create Board Risk and Audit Committee oversight processes | Low |
| W4 | Assign formal business-risk owners within each department | Low |
| W5 | Implement documented escalation procedures and thresholds | Low |
| W6 | Introduce Finance participation in security investment reviews | Low |
| W7 | Include Legal Counsel in governance committees | Low |
| W8 | Include HR in governance planning and awareness programmes | Low |
| W9 | Require executive approval for risk acceptance decisions | Low |
| W10 | Establish independent audit reporting channels | Low |
 
---
 
# Detailed Analysis
## W1: Concentrated Security Decision-Making
### Risk
A single individual controls governance decisions, creating a significant accountability concern.
 
### Corrective Control
Establish a Security Governance Committee and implement executive oversight.
 
### Residual Risk
Medium. Governance oversight reduces dependency on a single role.

---
 
## W2: Approval and Implementation Performed by Same Role
### Risk
Lack of independent review increases the possibility of ineffective controls.
 
### Corrective Control
Separate decision authority from implementation responsibilities.
 
### Residual Risk
Low.
 
---
 
## W3: Limited Board Involvement
### Risk
Material cyber risks may not receive sufficient strategic oversight.
 
### Corrective Control
Implement regular Board cyber-risk reporting.
 
### Residual Risk
Low.
 
---
 
## W4: Undefined Risk Ownership
### Risk
Business risks may remain unmanaged.
 
### Corrective Control
Assign risk ownership to Business Unit Leaders.
 
### Residual Risk
Low.
 
---
 
## W5: IT-Controlled Escalation
### Risk
Important incidents may not reach executive management in a timely manner.
 
### Corrective Control
Implement formal escalation thresholds and reporting requirements.
 
### Residual Risk
Low.
 
---
 
# Governance Recommendations
To sustain effective segregation of duties, TechGlobal should:
1. Maintain Board-level oversight.
2. Separate governance from operational implementation.
3. Apply formal accountability frameworks.
4. Maintain independent audit functions.
5. Conduct annual governance reviews.
6. Periodically assess governance maturity.
7. Review risk ownership assignments annually.
 
---
 
# Conclusion
Implementation of the proposed corrective controls significantly improves governance maturity, accountability, transparency, and segregation of duties across TechGlobal. Although residual risks remain, they are reduced to acceptable levels and can be managed through ongoing governance oversight, risk monitoring, and periodic review.
