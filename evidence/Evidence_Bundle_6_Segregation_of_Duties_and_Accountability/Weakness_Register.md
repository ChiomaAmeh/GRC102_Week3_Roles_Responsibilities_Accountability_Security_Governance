# Segregation of Duties and Accountability Weakness Register
## Purpose
This register identifies segregation-of-duties (SoD) and accountability weaknesses within TechGlobal's current IT-centric security governance model.

The register evaluates the associated risks, affected stakeholders, and governance implications.

---
 
# Weakness Register
| ID | Weakness | Associated Risk | Roles Involved |
|----|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| W1 | IT Director solely responsible for security decisions | Excessive concentration of authority and reduced governance oversight | IT Director |
| W2 | Same individual can approve and implement security controls | Lack of independent review and increased risk of errors | IT Director, IT Staff |
| W3 | No Board-level cyber-risk oversight | Significant risks may not receive strategic attention | Board, Executive Team |
| W4 | Risk ownership not formally assigned to business units | Accountability gaps and unmanaged business risks | Business Unit Leaders |
| W5 | Security incident escalation controlled exclusively by IT | Escalation delays and reduced visibility | IT Director |
| W6 | Security budget decisions concentrated within IT | Misalignment of investment priorities | IT Director, Finance |
| W7 | Limited Legal participation in governance decisions | Regulatory and compliance risks | Legal Counsel, IT |
| W8 | Human Resources excluded from governance activities | Weak workforce security governance | HR Director |
| W9 | Risk acceptance decisions performed by technical personnel | Conflicts of interest and poor risk governance | IT, Security Teams |
| W10 | Audit findings communicated through IT management | Reduced assurance independence | Internal Audit, IT |
 
---
 
# Governance Impact Analysis
The identified weaknesses create several governance challenges:
- Reduced accountability.
- Lack of independent oversight.
- Increased risk of biased decision-making.
- Inconsistent governance implementation.
- Poor executive visibility.
- Weak segregation of duties.
- Increased compliance exposure.
 
---
 
# Summary
The current governance model places excessive control within technical functions and lacks sufficient separation between governance, implementation, and oversight responsibilities.
